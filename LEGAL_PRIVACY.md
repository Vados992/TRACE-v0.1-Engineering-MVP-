# Legal and privacy deployment notes

These are operational design notes, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion. Actual lawful processing/access decisions belong to the installation's accountable controller and qualified advisers. Source publicity is not by itself a blanket permission to aggregate, republish or make adverse inferences about identifiable people.

Before using real personal data, document the purpose/legal basis, controller/processor roles, data categories, lawful register/API access, publisher license, contracts, retention, source freshness and rights of access/correction/appeal. Assess necessity, proportionality, vulnerable-person exposure and linkage/reidentification risk. Perform applicable privacy-impact assessment and institutional security review. Configure the operator contact and an actual rights/appeals workflow.

Collect the minimum necessary fields. Current live ownership mapping uses declared names and supplied registry references; it does not need birth dates, home addresses or identity documents. Some upstream responses can contain personal fields even if normalized rows omit them; protect the complete vault envelope accordingly and consider approved pre-storage minimization where legally required. Public output is explicit reviewed text, not a raw graph or evidence dump.

Keep license/source/date separate from evidence classification. BODS mapped publisher snapshots are not live authoritative registry mirrors; TED awarded sums are not spending/payment proof; GLEIF accounting parents do not establish natural-person ownership. A documented relationship or unexplained wealth residual does not establish a legal conflict or offense. Include uncertainty and provide correction/right-of-response mechanisms before adverse publication.

Use real credentials and access agreements for closed ownership, tax, bank, property or institutional procurement systems. This repository neither bypasses restrictions nor fabricates integrations with them. No connector is officially endorsed merely because an endpoint is public or executable.

Audit/retention and privacy erasure may conflict in particular contexts. Adopt an accountable lawful policy covering source objects, derived claims, case history, public withdrawals and backups; retain protected integrity records only as appropriate. No automatic permanent-erasure claim is made. Keep personal data, tokens and backups out of the public Git repository and CI artifacts.

Relevant primary references for legal review, not a conclusion about applicability: [EU GDPR text on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), [Open Ownership snapshot license/mapping notes](https://bods-data.openownership.org/source/uk_version_0_4/) and the specific publisher's access/license documentation. Validate current rules in the deployment jurisdiction.
