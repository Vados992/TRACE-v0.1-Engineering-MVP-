# Human review and anti-capture governance

The application produces inspectable source assertions and context. It does not issue corruption scores, automate adverse decisions or establish guilt. Independent reviewer and publisher actions are explicit API operations with recorded reasons and preserved history.

Creator, reviewer and publisher must have distinct identity subjects. The reviewer prepares public title/summary with necessary redaction; the publisher approves that exact text. An administrator cannot invoke reviewer/publisher powers through the admin role. Appeals immediately remove current public text while reopening review, and withdrawal leaves an accountable record. Claims can be retracted without destroying source history. Production forbids publication of synthetic case evidence.

Technical role separation alone cannot prove independence. Operators control API-key assignment and DB/storage administration. Institutional arrangements must include conflict disclosure, independent appointment/rotation of reviewers, documented recusals, review evidence, transparent corrections, rights of response and appeal, and meaningful oversight outside the data-holding institution.

Audit chains/case history resist ordinary application modification. A privileged DBA can disable triggers or rewrite the whole chain. Put signed audit checkpoints and immutable evidence copies under independent custody, separate signing authority from DB owners and maintain protected incident/appeal channels. Those organizational/off-host controls are deployment obligations; the application does not pretend they exist by shipping a placeholder signature or an imaginary regulator integration.

For source capture: retain publisher/version/time/URL/hash, do not turn a register omission into absence, show unresolved identities/unknown dates, review contradictory observations, and record parser changes. Re-evaluate stale snapshots before relying on them. Multi-supplier award values, ownership ranges and missing wealth components stay uncertain rather than becoming precise-looking unsupported facts.

For public release: verify relevance, lawful processing, source correctness, personal-data minimization, potential safety/reputational harm and right of response. Each installation must provide a real operator contact and appeals process. The public portal identifies the operator responsibility without inventing a government recipient or email address.
